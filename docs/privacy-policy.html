Privacy Policy

PurgeIt – Last updated: June 26, 2026

PurgeIt ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Storage data: PurgeIt scans your device's local storage to identify junk files, duplicate photos, and unused apps. This data never leaves your device.
  • Usage statistics: With your explicit permission (Usage Access), we read app usage timestamps to detect dormant apps. This data is processed locally only.
  • Crash reports: We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymised crash and error reports to improve app stability.
  • Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to understand feature usage in aggregate (e.g., how many users open the Photo Cleaner). No personally identifiable information is collected.
  • Purchase information: Subscriptions are processed by Google Play and validated by RevenueCat. We do not store your payment details.

2. Permissions Used

  • MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Required to scan all files for junk and duplicates.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: Required to scan photos for duplicates and low-quality images.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: Required to identify apps you haven't used recently.
  • INTERNET: Required for Firebase and RevenueCat services.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required to restart scheduled scans after device reboot.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data may be shared with:

  • Google Firebase – for crash reporting and anonymous analytics.
  • RevenueCat – for subscription management and validation.
  • Google Play – for payment processing.

4. Data Retention

All file scan results are stored locally on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Firebase Analytics data is retained for 14 months per Google's standard policy.

5. Children's Privacy

PurgeIt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to access, correct, and erase personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via an in-app notice.

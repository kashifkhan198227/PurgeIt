Privacy Policy

PurgeIt  ·  Last updated: June 26, 2026

🔒 Your data stays on your device

This Privacy Policy describes how PurgeIt ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information. PurgeIt is an Android storage cleaner and file management application.

1. Information We Collect

PurgeIt processes files and storage data locally on your device only. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information to external servers. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required
  • No personal data is sent to our servers
  • No location data is accessed
  • No contacts or call logs are accessed
  • File scanning is performed entirely on-device

2. Storage Access

PurgeIt requires access to your device storage to scan for large files, duplicates, old downloads, and cache data. This access is used solely within the app to display results and delete files you explicitly select. No file contents are read, uploaded, or shared.

3. Usage Statistics

The app may use Android's UsageStatsManager API to display app storage usage. This data is accessed locally and never leaves your device.

4. Third-Party Services

PurgeIt integrates the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (Google) – collects anonymous crash reports and usage analytics to help us improve the app. No personally identifiable information is collected. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat – manages in-app subscriptions and purchase validation. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing – processes in-app purchases. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

PurgeIt is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Deletion

Files deleted through PurgeIt are permanently removed from your device. This action cannot be undone. We recommend reviewing selections carefully before confirming deletions.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
